I have never seen anything quite like this place. Even the brightest fire could not light this place more than a couple meters. It is hard just writing this note. 
All my companions are gone. They started muttering to themselves before running off into the dark. I have started to hear voices calling out to me. I fear I might suffer the same fate as the rest of them. If I don’t come back then please. To whoever poor fool is reading this. Turn back. Whatever it is that's hiding in here is not worth it.  

-Henrik Alfson, 1398
