Diary of Yvonne Joergenson

I got called down here to Gunhildr to look at a dungeon they apparently call “Agathol”, awfully odd name for such a place but who am I to judge? No matter though, after all I’ve gotten offered a pretty penny to look at the traps that apparently are housed within and disarm them. Seems easy enough. Place seems old from what I've heard so it shouldn’t be anything complicated. 

TURNPAGE Turns out, I was half right. The traps here are mostly obvious and easy to spot however they are brutal and I can't find any way to disarm them permanently. Whenever I cut one of the tripwires they assemble themselves back again as soon as I look away. These traps definitely aren’t normal, and I assume there’s some sort of magic trickery with it. In other words, this is not a job for me. Now I just gotta get out before I accidentally step on one of them.
