William Regin’s notebook

“Chapter 4, The Scorched Sword.”
The Great dragon Xayoc was once said to have forged a blade of pure flame that he dubbed “The Scorched Sword” which he later threw away into the depths of an unknown crypt when he was driven back in the War of the Mountain. 
I believe that there is no better option than for him to have hidden it in Agathol. Or it is at least the best option. No other similar “crypts” have ever been discovered near where said conflict was fought and if all of my family's stories are to be believed then that place seems like a perfect spot to hide such a relic.
However, most of them seem to dismiss my theory. “There may be treasure down there but not that of fairy tales” they say. Even so, you can't prove a negative. I will descend into Agathol like my forefathers before me and prove them all wrong.
