Edwards journal. 
Entry 15
The numerous tales of the so-called “Agathol” have led me to the city of Günhildr where it was said to lie. It turns out the rumors were true. This place is crawling with seasoned and amateur explorers alike, all roaring to get to the bottom of the dungeon and loot whatever treasures are hidden within. Although I have heard some more concerning tales as well. Apparently people rarely return. and the few that do often aren't too happy to talk about it. 
I can't quite wrap my head around why so many people are risking their lives to explore that place. Although, I myself have gotten a strange inclination to do the same.
