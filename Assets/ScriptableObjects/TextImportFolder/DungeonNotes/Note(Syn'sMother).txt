To Syn

Forgive me Syn. Your mother has been very foolish.
I know you will probably never see this but on the off chance you do I need you to know I’m very proud of you. Even though you hide your feelings behind your harsh words, I can tell that you really do care about the people around you. And I think they know too. 
Yet I still went down here thinking I’d find something. And I can’t even remember what it was.
Syn.. I’m sorry. I’m sorry that I’ll never get to see you again. I’m sorry that I couldn’t be a better mother. Do tell your father that I love him okay? And of course.
I love you Syn. I hope you live a long and happy life.
