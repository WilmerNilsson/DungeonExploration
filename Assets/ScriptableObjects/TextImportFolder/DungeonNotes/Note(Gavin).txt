To any unfortunate fool that somehow sees this, please listen.
These things are more powerful than I thought. Brute strength won’t save you against them. However they are sloppy and predictable. If you manage to parry their attacks then they are likely to stagger in shock, just be sure to strike them back when you have the chance. And if you feel overwhelmed, run, get out of this damn place when you still have a mind to keep. Don't be like me. Don't die for nothing.
