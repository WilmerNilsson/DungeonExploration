”Log entry 18”

None of the anecdotes could have prepared me for this place. I had heard from a previous adventurer that this place was wicked but that proved to be an understatement. I’ve seen countless people down here. But they all act like wandering corpses. They mutter nonsense and go on and on about Agathol. Some even bolted for me when they noticed my presence but I somehow managed to escape.
I believe it’s safe to assume that ”Agathol” has possessed them somehow. And I have a hypothesis as to what might be causing it. The dark.
Whenever my torch flickers I can hear faint voices calling out to me. Calling out ”Agathol”.
I pray I can get out of here before my only light goes out.
